Issue: tstack/lnav #1741

Environment:
Linux Mint / Ubuntu 24.04 base

Observed:
1. Interactive command:
   :goto 4000
   Result: works, lands at L4000 (~80%).

2. filename:line syntax:
   lnav synthetic-500.log:400
   Result: opens near end of file, L499 / 100%.

Conclusion:
The two navigation methods behave differently in this synthetic syslog test.

3. Command-line goto:
   lnav -c ':goto 4000' synthetic.log
   Result: does not land at line 4000.
   Observed position: approximately L4990 / 100%.

Conclusion:
Interactive :goto behaves differently from command-line -c ':goto NNN'
and filename:line navigation with the synthetic syslog test.
